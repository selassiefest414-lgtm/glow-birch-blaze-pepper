# Rainbow DJ Lab institutional blueprint

Ras Tafari Inc. leadership: I recommend launching Rainbow DJ Lab as a rigorous **noncredit community-arts program**, pursuing college recognition for assessed student work through a separate agreement, and choosing an accreditation route once the Lab can demonstrate sustained instruction and student outcomes. The attached **Rainbow DJ Lab: Academic Governance, Endowment, and Institutional Prestige Blueprint** gives your Board a draft advisory charter, a 120-hour pilot curriculum and capstone rubric, a five-year fundraising plan, and facilities and faculty standards.

## The decisions that shape the launch

1. **Give advisors influence without giving them corporate authority.** The proposed nine-member Academic & Industry Advisory Board brings together Chicago house culture bearers, music educators, producers, industry leaders, and a learner representative. It reviews curriculum and student work and reports to the corporate Board, which retains authority over policy, budgets, contracts, and accreditation.

2. **Pursue accreditation for the institution you intend to operate.** ACCPAS is the better fit if Rainbow remains a community-arts school; NASM’s professional non-degree route calls for a substantially larger postsecondary program. If the applicable freestanding standards apply, either route would require expanding the current three-director governing board to **at least five**; advisory seats do not count ([ACCPAS Handbook, Appendix I.A](https://accpas.arts-accredit.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/6/2026/02/ACCPAS-2026-Handbook-Final-02-20-2026.pdf); [NASM Handbook, XXI.1.D](https://nasm.arts-accredit.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/02/M-2025-26-Handbook-Final-02-24-2026.pdf)). Make the certificate verifiable and portfolio-backed, but leave any academic credit award to the receiving college. [nasm.arts-accredit](https://nasm.arts-accredit.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/02/M-2025-26-Handbook-Final-02-24-2026.pdf)

3. **Build the endowment without depending on it to launch.** The blueprint models **$1 million in collected permanent gifts over five years** as a working goal, not a forecast. At an illustrative 4% payout, that valuation base would support about $40,000 annually, subject to donor terms and a documented Board decision on prudent spending. Fund operations, equipment, and current-use scholarships separately. [docs.legis.wisconsin](https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/statutes/statutes/112/11)

4. **Invest first in teaching and supervised practice.** Start with one club-standard reference booth, smaller practice stations, and a stereo production lab. Hire master practitioners through paid teaching auditions, and use a partner studio for immersive audio before committing to a dedicated build.

**First Board action:** Verify the signed versions of Articles IX and XI, assign an academic lead, and seek a written Wisconsin determination before opening enrollment. The draft uses your description of the bylaws because I couldn’t retrieve the signed copy; counsel should check the proposed charters and delegations against it before adoption.
